User guide

Getting started

Press Start to begin timing. The display counts minutes, seconds, and hundredths of a second while the stopwatch runs.

Start, pause & resume

Start begins timing from zero. Once running, the same button becomes Pause, which freezes the display without losing your time. Press it again — now labelled Resume — to continue from exactly where you left off.

Recording laps

Press Lap while the stopwatch is running to record a split. Split is the time since the previous lap; Total is the time since Start. The fastest and slowest splits are marked once you have three or more laps.

Resetting

Reset stops the stopwatch, clears the elapsed time back to 00:00.00, and removes all recorded laps. Use Clear in the splits panel if you only want to remove the lap list without resetting the clock.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Space Start / pause / resume
  • L Record a lap
  • R Reset the stopwatch

Light & dark mode

Use the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner to switch themes. Your preference is remembered on this device.
